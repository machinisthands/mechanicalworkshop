Insert a part

Open › to insert a part. Drag parts into place. Use Line to join the ports.

Draw

  1. Press Draw first (until then the page scrolls normally over the sheet).
  2. Tap the › arrow on the left of the sheet and choose a part; drag it into place.
  3. Press Line: all free ports light up green. Press on one and drag to another (or tap one, then the other).
  4. Tap a line or a part, then Delete.
  5. Plug (in the › list): tap a port to put a plug in; tap it again to take it out.

Simulate

  1. Press Simulate: the compressor runs.
  2. Tap the sliding valve to open it.
  3. Tap the 5/2 lever valve to change over.
  4. Lines under pressure turn blue; an open port shows a red warning.
